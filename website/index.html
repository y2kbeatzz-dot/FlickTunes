More than a file transfer.

RIFT combines a local, paired connection with a little magic on both screens.

🖼️

Photos & videos

Choose from Photos. Swipe left and see a visual arrival on your Mac.

📁

Files & documents

Pick from Files and receive them in your Mac's Downloads/RIFT folder.

🔗

Links & clipboard

Send a link or copied text on purpose. Links won't open on your Mac without a click.

How it works

Pair once. Then just send.

Open RIFT on Mac

Keep the Mac receiver running in the menu bar. Approve your iPhone the first time.

Choose your item

In the RIFT iPhone app, pick a photo, video or file, or enter a web link and pick your arrival effect.

Drag left to send

Swipe the item off the iPhone's left edge. The Mac saves it and displays the chosen portal arrival.

Download RIFT

Your devices. Your portal.

Get the Mac receiver and iPhone sender. Both apps are required for nearby handoff.
