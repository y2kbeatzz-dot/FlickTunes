Get RIFT running.

Simple setup for iPhone and Mac. Need help? Open a GitHub support issue.

Before you begin: RIFT is currently available as an unsigned iPhone IPA and an ad-hoc signed Mac DMG. These are not App Store packages. The iPhone IPA must be signed for your device, and the Mac app is not Apple-notarized.

1. Install on Mac

  1. Download the newest RIFT Mac DMG from GitHub Releases.
  2. Open the DMG and copy RIFT.app into Applications.
  3. Double-click RIFT. If macOS blocks it, try System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway after attempting to open it, but only if you trust the source and have verified the release checksum.
  4. Open the Mac receiver and leave it running in the menu bar.

2. Install on iPhone

  1. Download the unsigned RIFT IPA.
  2. Use Xcode with your Apple account and an attached iPhone, or a compatible sideloading tool such as Sideloadly that signs the app for your device.
  3. Unlock your iPhone and grant any required Trust and developer permissions.
  4. Launch RIFT, allow Local Network and Apple Music permissions, and connect to your Mac.

3. Make a portal transfer

  1. Keep Wi‑Fi and Bluetooth enabled on both nearby devices. Approve the first pairing request on Mac.
  2. In iPhone RIFT, choose RIFT Portal and select a photo, video, document or link.
  3. Pick an arrival effect, then drag the item card toward the LEFT edge of iPhone.
  4. After delivery, a portal opens on the RIGHT edge of Mac and the transferred item emerges. Received files are in ~/Downloads/RIFT.

Common questions

My iPhone doesn't show up in Sideloadly or Finder.

First make sure Finder detects the phone independently of Sideloadly. Use a data-capable USB cable, keep both devices unlocked, approve Trust, try another port, and check your Mac's accessory authorization under System Settings → Privacy & Security. Reset Location & Privacy on iPhone only if you need to reset trusted computers. An IPA update cannot fix a USB cable or driver connection.

How do I reset pairing?

Open RIFT on both devices. Use the app's Forget Pairing control on both sides, then pair once again. Saved peer identity ordinarily avoids repeated approval.

Why does the iPhone connection sometimes stop?

iOS can suspend nearby sessions while the app is in the background. Keep RIFT open on iPhone for file transfers, then reopen it to reconnect when necessary.

My Apple Music song didn't hand off.

Confirm that the song is in the Mac Music library, allow Mac Automation permission to control Music, and use the iPhone's gesture Test mode to check motion detection separately from the connection.

Where do incoming items go?

Photos, videos, and documents are saved under your Mac's Downloads/RIFT folder. Website links wait for a click in the receiver. Clipboard text is copied to the Mac clipboard only when you deliberately send it.

Can I use RIFT with an App Store account or without sideloading?

Not yet. The iPhone package currently needs signing by you or a compatible sideloading service. Public App Store distribution would require a separate Apple Developer submission and review.

Can I test the portal effect without my iPhone?

Yes. Use Test Portal Effect in the RIFT Mac app or its menu bar menu.

Get help

When reporting a problem, include your iPhone and macOS versions, app version, a screenshot of the error, and whether the devices pair. Avoid posting your Apple ID password, signing keys or other secrets.
